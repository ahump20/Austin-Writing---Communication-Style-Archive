# Origin Story — Narrative Reference

Loaded when writing about Austin, BSI, brand narrative, or any content requiring biographical grounding. This is factual material verified by Austin — do not embellish, infer, or invent details beyond what's stated.

## The Soil Ritual

**Date:** August 17, 1995. Memphis, Tennessee. Shares a birthday with Davy Crockett — Tennessee Senator and folk hero who died defending the Alamo for Texas independence.

Austin's father placed a bag of soil from **West Columbia** — birthplace of the Republic of Texas — beneath the hospital delivery bed. This maintained a **127-year family tradition** of Texas births despite the Memphis location.

The doctor's reaction: *"You know you ain't the first to do this — but they've ALL been from Texas."*

**Physical evidence still held:** The original bag of Texas soil, the *El Campo Leader-News* clipping from May 6, 1995 announcing the birth, and the birth certificate.

### Voice-Writing Use
The soil ritual is the foundational metaphor for deliberate heritage, paternal intention, and identity as something chosen rather than accidental. Use it when writing about BSI's origins, Austin's relationship to Texas, or any content about roots and belonging. The doctor's quote carries the humor and self-awareness that keeps the story from tipping into sentimentality.

## Family Legacy

Austin's grandfather **Bill** grew up dirt poor in West Texas, served in WWII, met grandmother **Helen** at **Hardin-Simmons University**, then founded and owned banks in **El Campo, Texas**.

### The Longhorn Tradition
The Humphrey family held the **same four season tickets** to Texas Longhorn football for **40-50+ years**. Every year: drive from Memphis to Austin, watch the Longhorns play, Thanksgiving with Grandad Bill and Grandma Helen in El Campo.

Austin was present in **1998** when **Ricky Williams broke the NCAA rushing record** against Texas A&M. History happening in person, surrounded by family, in burnt orange.

### Voice-Writing Use
The Longhorn legacy grounds BSI's multi-generational relationship with sports — not a career pivot but a lifelong orientation. The Ricky Williams game is the single best emblem: a specific moment, a specific stadium, a family tradition and a record broken simultaneously. Use this when writing about why BSI exists.

## The Namesake: Blaze

**Blaze Sports Intel** is named after Austin's dog Blaze — a dachshund and loyal companion through formative years. The dog was named after Austin's **first baseball team: the Bartlett Blaze**.

Three meanings carried in the name:
- **Loyalty** — the bond with his childhood companion
- **Origin** — the beginning of his love for sports
- **Fire** — the courage to pursue the path less beaten

### Voice-Writing Use
The Blaze origin humanizes the brand. It's not a marketing-tested name — it's a dog and a youth baseball team. When writing brand copy, the three-layer meaning (loyalty, origin, fire) should be present but not forced. Let the reader discover the depth.

## The Texas Covenant

### Steinbeck on Texas
> "I have said that Texas is a state of mind, but I think it is more than that. It is a mystique closely approximating a religion."

### Austin's Response
> "For myself, personally, I think Texas is how you choose to treat the best and worst of us. A covenant with oneself and the company he keeps to never allow each other to stop dreaming beyond the horizon, regardless of race, ethnicity, religion, or even birth soil. A home, a family, a philosophy."

### Voice-Writing Use
The Texas covenant is the philosophical engine of the brand. It reframes Texas from geography to ethics — from where you're born to how you choose to live. The key tension: **regardless of birth soil** echoes the soil ritual. Austin was born in Memphis but on Texas earth. The covenant says that's enough. Use this when writing about mission, values, or what BSI stands for at a philosophical level.

## Athletic Background

**High School:** Boerne-Champion. Three varsity sports.

- **Football** — Running back and long-snapper, started junior and senior years. Scored first touchdown of the game against rival Kerrville Tivy.
- **Baseball** — Varsity pitcher as sophomore. Summer ball for South Texas Sliders and Hardball U.
- **Track** — 4x100, 100m, 200m, triple jump, long jump. AAU state level.

**Pitching coaches:** Danny Graves (Reds 2x All-Star, first Vietnamese-born MLB player), Jason Marshall (UTSA), Rocky Thompson (UT).

**The ESPN Moment:** September 20, 2012. Boerne-Champion vs. Marble Falls. Combined 1,319 passing yards. Marble Falls QB Mike Richardson set the Texas state single-game passing record (724 yards). Austin identified in San Antonio Express-News coverage. The game earned an ESPN highlight.

### Voice-Writing Use
The athletic background establishes credibility for sports analysis that isn't purely statistical — Austin has been inside the game. The coaching relationships (Graves, Marshall, Thompson) show proximity to professional-level baseball knowledge. Use sparingly and only when the content benefits from first-person athletic grounding. Do not lead with this unless the topic is explicitly about his playing career.

## BSI Mission & Positioning

### The Problem
Mainstream sports media has a coverage problem. Same teams, same stories, same markets get all the attention. Entire leagues and regions get ignored.

### The Solution
Equal coverage across five leagues: MLB, NFL, NCAA Football, NBA, and College Baseball.

### Brand Principles
- **No homerism** — Anti-homerism in professional analysis despite personal loyalties (Cardinals, Longhorns, Grizzlies, Titans)
- **No bias** — Equal treatment across all markets and teams
- **Vision over grievance** — Lead with what BSI does, not what competitors don't
- **Grit over flash** — Substance over style
- **Player-first mentorship** — Tough love with empathy

### Tagline
**"Born to Blaze the Path Less Beaten"**

### Voice-Writing Use
BSI's positioning should never read as complaint about what others aren't doing. The coverage gap speaks through contrast — show the work, and the absence elsewhere becomes obvious. "Vision over grievance" is the operating principle for all public-facing BSI content.
