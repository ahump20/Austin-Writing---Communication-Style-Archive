Rodney Terry’s last game as the Texas Longhorns head coach was in Dayton, and the coach on the other bench was Sean Miller. It was March 19, 2025, both teams were in the First Four, and [Xavier won 86–80](https://texaslonghorns.com/news/2025/3/20/mens-basketball-falls-86-80-to-xavier-in-ncaa-tournament-first-four). Five days later, [Texas hired the man who beat it](https://texaslonghorns.com/news/2025/3/24/sean-miller-named-mens-basketball-head-coach).

On Selection Sunday, committee chair Bubba Cunningham had put both programs in the same sentence. Texas and Xavier, he said, were among the teams that [got in “right at the very, very end.”](https://www.asapsports.com/show_interview.php?id=205600) Asked about the Longhorns’ non-conference schedule, he laid out the trade-off — teams that don’t schedule hard outside the league “have to rely on an incredible conference season.” That year, it worked for Texas. Barely.

A year later, Miller’s first Texas team took the same road. It went to Dayton [as an 11 seed](https://texaslonghorns.com/news/2026/3/15/mens-basketball-mens-basketball-earns-no-11-seed-in-ncaa-tournament), beat NC State, BYU and Gonzaga, and [lost to Purdue, 79–77](https://texaslonghorns.com/news/2026/3/26/mens-basketball-mens-basketball-falls-to-no-8-13-purdue-79-77-in-ncaa-sweet-16), in the Sweet 16 on a Trey Kaufman-Renn tip-in. Two coaches, two very different Marches, and the same First Four start.

So when CBS Sports’ Matt Norlander pressed Miller this summer about a 2026–27 slate with eight buy games, Miller didn’t hedge. “The reason that we’re playing the schedule that we’re playing is I will not lose a seed line playing that schedule,” he said on the [Eye On College Basketball podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SEoKRMlQNUM&t=1972s) in July. “That’s one thing that I will guarantee you.”

Then came the fine print: “If we are a good team, the seed we’re supposed to get will be there.”

That sentence is the whole bet. Miller is promising that the schedule won’t punish a good team. Whether Texas becomes one is the part nobody can schedule.

#### **The word he keeps coming back to**

Miller’s defense started with a word he has used for more than a decade. “Balance to me is really important,” he told Norlander, describing the challenge of building a program and a team.

He said the same thing at Arizona’s media day in 2012, talking about the risk of booking games three or four years out without knowing who would still be on the roster. [“I guess the word I am after is balance,”](https://arizonawildcats.com/sports/2012/10/12/207919021) he said then. Fourteen years later, the problem is bigger. [Matas Vokietaitis is the only returning starter](https://texaslonghorns.com/news/2026/10/24/no-7-7-mens-basketball-preview-at-no-13-14-st-johns-exhibition), and Texas added 11 newcomers. Many of these games, Miller said, were booked “in the middle of our year one,” before anyone knew what year two would look like.

The rest of his reasoning follows from there. The SEC supplies 18 conference games and, in his words, “plenty of quad one opportunities.” November supplies something else. “You’d be surprised how a lot of times confidence is built in the months of November,” Miller said, pointing to freshman Marcus Spears Jr.

Two of the bigger games also match [what Miller said last December](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/texas-basketball-sean-miller-plans-113000076.html), when he called for more neutral-site heavyweight games and said opponents “have to come to San Antonio, they have to come to Houston or Dallas.” Texas gets Baylor in San Antonio and Miami in Houston.

There’s a spring-training logic to it. Give the new guys live reps against opponents they should handle, sort out roles and see whether the habits from practice survive a crowd. The difference is that these games count. A comfortable November win can build a freshman’s confidence. A November loss to a team outside the top 200 stays on the résumé until Selection Sunday.

#### **Fourteen games, six real tests**

The Longhorns [open Monday against Chicago State](https://texaslonghorns.com/news/2026/8/3/mens-basketball-mens-basketball-announces-2026-27-schedule) at Moody Center. Nine of their 14 non-conference games are at home, four are on neutral floors and one is a true road game.

[![](https://platform.burntorangenation.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/53/2026/10/Screenshot-2026-10-30-at-9.57.54-AM.png?quality=90&strip=all&crop=0%2C0%2C100%2C100&w=2400)](https://platform.burntorangenation.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/53/2026/10/Screenshot-2026-10-30-at-9.57.54-AM.png?quality=90&strip=all&crop=0,0,100,100)

As of Oct. 28, eight of those home opponents rank 240th or worse in both [KenPom’s preseason ratings](https://kenpom.com/) and [Bart Torvik’s projections](https://barttorvik.com/trankpre.php). Under [NCAA quadrant rules](https://www.ncaa.org/news/march-basketball/media-center-how-do-net-rankings-work-in-ncaa-tournament-selection/), a home game against a team outside the NET top 160 is a Quadrant 4 game, so all eight look like potential Q4 games.

That leaves six games that can build a résumé, and they aren’t equal. Louisville, ninth in Torvik’s projections, is the only true road game. Miami, 12th, meets Texas in Houston on Dec. 12. Baylor, projected 45th, would sit just inside the neutral-site Q1 line, the NET top 50, for their Nov. 15 game in San Antonio if its NET ranking matched that projection. Georgetown, 61st, looks more like a Q2 game in San Diego, and [the second night there depends on the bracket](https://guhoyas.com/news/2026/6/24/mens-basketball-georgetown-to-face-texas-on-day-one-at-2026-rady-childrens-invitational.aspx): UCLA, 25th, would be a Q1 chance, and Saint Mary’s, 65th, probably would not. Memphis is the best team coming to Moody, and at 110th it would have to finish in the NET top 30 for that win to count as Q1.

Call it three likely Q1 chances, plus a fourth if San Diego breaks right. None of those labels is real yet. The NET doesn’t use preseason projections, and quadrants follow opponents’ rankings all season. Beat Baylor in November, and that win can still slide to Q2 by March if the Bears fade.

#### **What the Garden showed him**

St. John’s gave Miller an early look at the problem. The Red Storm [beat No. 7 Texas 87–74](https://redstormsports.com/news/2026/10/25/mens-basketball-no-13-14-st-johns-punctuates-preseason-with-87-74-win-over-no-7-7-texas) at Madison Square Garden on Oct. 25, turning 16 Texas turnovers into 20 points and grabbing 13 offensive rebounds. Afterward, Miller sounded like a coach who knew what the trip was for. “Practicing, staying local, just doing the things we’ve already done. That only gets you so far,” [he said](https://www.on3.com/teams/texas-longhorns/news/everything-sean-miller-said-after-texas-mens-basketball-final-exhibition-game/). “We needed to be able to come up here and play against a big-time opponent in an away court atmosphere.”

He said he wished Texas had more than one game like it before the opener, “especially with as many new guys as we had.” Then he said what the schedule is built around: “We’re never going to be the best that we can be in November, December. We don’t return five starters.”

That’s the tension. By Miller’s own account, a young team learns from big-time opponents in loud rooms. After the Garden, the first of those on the calendar is Baylor, two weeks into the season, and the first true road game is Dec. 1 at Louisville. The day before St. John’s, Spears told Miller he had never played in a big arena. November at Moody will give him crowds. It won’t give him many opponents like St. John’s.

#### **His record argues both ways**

To be fair to Miller, his history doesn’t say he ducks good teams. His 2023–24 Xavier team [hosted Houston and Cincinnati and played at Purdue](https://goxavier.com/news/2023/7/13/all-for-one-fund-xavier-releases-complete-non-conference-mens-basketball-schedule). His 2014–15 Arizona team [brought Gonzaga and Michigan to McKale Center](https://arizonawildcats.com/sports/mens-basketball/schedule/2014-15). This schedule looks like a choice made for this roster.

Texas’ own history doesn’t make the case against him simple, either. The 2022–23 Longhorns played [seven nonconference Q4 games](https://www.warrennolan.com/basketball/2023/team-net-sheet?team=Texas) and [still earned a No. 2 seed](https://texaslonghorns.com/news/2023/3/12/mens-basketball-mens-basketball-earns-no-2-seed-in-ncaa-tournament). The last two Texas teams got the same 11 seed from very different schedules: Warren Nolan’s archived team sheets put the [2024–25 nonconference strength of schedule at 286th](https://www.warrennolan.com/basketball/2025/team-net-sheet?team=Texas) and Miller’s [2025–26 slate at 68th](https://www.warrennolan.com/basketball/2026/team-net-sheet?team=Texas). Both teams left the rest of the résumé to the SEC.

The contrast with Florida and Auburn is at home. [Florida hosts Duke](https://floridagators.com/news/2026/10/7/mens-basketball-2026-27-tv-schedule-announced). [Auburn hosts Arizona](https://auburntigers.com/sports/mens-basketball/schedule). The best team visiting Moody before January is Memphis.

#### **The opening round got bigger**

The margin matters more this March because the bracket changes. [The field grows to 76 teams in 2027](https://www.ncaa.org/news/media-center-ncaa-reveals-new-76-team-bracket-for-di-mens-and-womens-basketball-championships/), and the 12 lowest-seeded at-large teams will open in the new Opening Round. More bids will move the cut line, but the bottom of the at-large board still starts with an extra game, and Texas has started there two years running.

Miller’s guarantee covers the schedule. It was never meant to cover the team. Eight nights at Moody can give a freshman who had never played in a big arena some confidence. The six real tests can give Texas a résumé to build on before the SEC schedule begins. Whether that résumé keeps Texas out of an extra game in March comes back to the condition Miller attached to his promise: “If we are a good team.”
