The Texas Longhorns [released their full 2027 baseball schedule Monday](https://texaslonghorns.com/news/2026/11/9/baseball-unveils-2027-schedule), with 54 regular-season games and 35 at UFCU Disch-Falk Field.

“We’re looking forward to the competition this schedule brings,” Texas head coach Jim Schlossnagle said. “Between our SEC opponents and the non-conference games, we’ll have plenty of opportunities to challenge ourselves against some really good teams. Our goal is to continue getting better throughout the season and put ourselves in the best position possible when the postseason comes around.”

Jim Schlossnagle’s team opens Feb. 19 against Clemson at Globe Life Field in the Shriners Children’s College Showdown. Texas faces TCU the following day and Virginia on Feb. 21 before returning to Austin for its home opener against Lamar on Feb. 23.

That starts a 13-game homestand — Washington State, Loyola Marymount and Murray State visit for weekend series, with Texas Southern, Texas A&M-Corpus Christi and Tarleton State filling out the midweek schedule before SEC play.

The Longhorns open their 30-game conference schedule at LSU on March 19-21. South Carolina comes to Austin on March 25-27 for the first home SEC series.

Tennessee, Arkansas, Vanderbilt and Texas A&M round out the home conference slate. The Aggies visit for the Cotton Holdings Lone Star Showdown, the final home weekend of the regular season.

Texas also travels to Alabama, Florida and Oklahoma before closing the regular season at Georgia on May 20-22. Texas State is the Longhorns’ only nonconference road opponent, with a March 23 game in San Marcos and a return visit to Austin on April 20.

The schedule includes 26 games against 11 teams that reached the 2026 NCAA Tournament, according to Texas.

Home games are at UFCU Disch-Falk Field, and the three Arlington games air on FloSports. All dates and times are subject to change.
