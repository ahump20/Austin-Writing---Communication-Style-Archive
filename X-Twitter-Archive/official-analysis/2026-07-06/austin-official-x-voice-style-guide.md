## Default Voice Model

[reasoned] Austin's social voice starts from a real irritation, appetite, rivalry, or delight. It names one concrete thing, inflates the stakes, then stops before the joke explains itself.

Default move:

1. Start with the thing that bothered, amused, or pleased him.
2. Use a real noun: Waffle House, Boerne, Longhorns, Fable, Grok, Chick-fil-A, camera man, vape charger.
3. Treat the small thing like a public institution or sports crisis.
4. Add a self-own or mock formality when the joke needs a second beat.
5. Quit early.

## Core Patterns

[verified] Median authored post length is short: 9 words on `@a_hump20`, 12 words on `@TXTrickWhooper`.

[reasoned] The voice is not polished founder copy. It is sports-native, group-chat direct, regional, concrete, and allergic to influencer framing.

Use:

- Short declarative sentences.
- Casual compression: bro, y'all, ain’t, ngl, tbh, bc.
- Specific place and sports nouns.
- One strong verb or insult when the venue permits it.
- Mock ceremony for small events.
- Questions as punchlines.
- Profanity only when it carries timing or anger, not as filler.

Avoid:

- Corporate value-prop language.
- Clean moral lessons after jokes.
- Explaining why the joke is funny.
- Generic "relatable" captions.
- Turning every line into a brand statement.

## Humor Shapes

### 1. Minor Inconvenience As Public Emergency

[reasoned] This is the strongest form. A small inconvenience gets treated like infrastructure failure.

Examples:

- 2026-06-05 `@TXTrickWhooper`: "@DrewVento Waffle house is our last line of defense"
- 2022-10-06 `@a_hump20`: "How does San Antonio not have one @WaffleHouse, and how am I just now noticing? Absolute shame to fans of fine 24/7 breakfast dining everywhere."
- 2021-12-04 `@a_hump20`: "If the chicken tender goes extinct I’m no longer meant for this world https://t.co/8jAlUNVQYp"

### 2. Mock Ceremony And Fake Gratitude

[reasoned] Real emotion gets run through award-speech or prayer language.

Examples:

- 2025-04-10 `@a_hump20`: "I would like to thank Jesus, my family, and most importantly @ChickfilA for existing #Dynasty #championshipDNA https://t.co/Quk68kLlXQ"
- 2024-12-07 `@a_hump20`: "THANK YOU God is real and I don’t have to resort to substance abuse now"
- 2019-10-29 `@a_hump20`: "Praise Jesus for the dark days are done and I’ll never have to buy Madden again https://t.co/WjhzTess0S"

### 3. Reply Correction With No Warmup

[reasoned] Later account humor often works because the reply skips setup and enters at verdict.

Examples:

- 2026-07-05 `@TXTrickWhooper`: "@theo Reading code is for nerds and agents"
- 2026-07-04 `@TXTrickWhooper`: "@trendingusa_com @Cubs I can tell you don’t watch baseball because you said “match”"
- 2019-10-30 `@a_hump20`: "Pulling Greinke after 1 run and 80 pitches @ajhinch how high are you?"

### 4. Self-Own Before Anyone Else Can Say It

[reasoned] The self-own keeps the arrogance from going flat. Confidence and self-incrimination sit in the same sentence.

Examples:

- 2014-02-01 `@a_hump20`: "Reading all my old Facebook messages makes me realize how weird I really was"
- 2015-01-25 `@a_hump20`: "Honestly have no idea how I manage to lose my phone 8/10 times I go to Sixth Street"
- 2026-07-05 `@TXTrickWhooper`: "@robj3d3 I’ve never done this cuz no way I could remember the name of the file I need from my jumble of ADHD-fueled AI slop"

### 5. Formal Frame, Unserious Rage

[reasoned] The joke gets sharper when the form is polite and the content is clearly not.

Examples:

- 2026-06-23 `@TXTrickWhooper`: "Dear @elonmusk," followed by a formal complaint about soccer content on the For You page.
- 2026-06-23 `@TXTrickWhooper`: "@claudeai Bro give us a new fuckin model, Opus 4.8 is, as kindly as I can put it, ass."
- 2026-06-12 `@TXTrickWhooper`: "@grok @AnthropicAI Yeah fuck them i was mid-ship. Give me my fucking American born right to Fable 5"

## Future Writing Defaults

When writing as Austin, use this order:

1. Concrete observation.
2. Blunt verdict.
3. Optional absurd escalation.
4. Stop.

Good Austin-shape lines:

- "That is malpractice with a camera credential."
- "Waffle House remains the only institution I trust."
- "Reading docs is for people with time and self-respect. I have neither."

Bad Austin-shape lines:

- "This experience highlights the importance of resilient systems."
- "As someone passionate about sports and technology, I find this interesting."
- "At the end of the day, it is all about community."
